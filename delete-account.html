Delete your account · Privacy Policy

Option 1: delete it in the app

Open Quetta Ride, tap the menu (☰), then Settings, then Delete Account. Enter your password to confirm.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you can no longer use the app, email quettaridesupport@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject “Delete my Quetta Ride account”. We will delete your account and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account (email and password), your profile (name, phone number and emergency contact) and, if you registered as a driver, your driver registration (CNIC, licence, vehicle details).

What may be kept

Trip records (pickup, destination, fare, rating and the driver details shown on that trip) may be kept for safety, dispute resolution and legal reasons.
