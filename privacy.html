Quetta Ride

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 17 October 2026 · Terms of Service

1. Introduction

Quetta Ride ("we", "us", "the app") connects riders with drivers for rides in and around Quetta, Pakistan. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Account information: your name, email address and password (the password is handled by our sign-in provider and is never stored by us in plain text), and your phone number if you add one. Location: if you allow location access, we use your device location to suggest your pickup point. The pickup and destination coordinates are saved with each ride request. While a driver has an accepted ride, the app shares the driver's live location (while the app is open) with the rider of that ride so the rider can see them arrive; it stops when the ride ends. Trip information: pickup and destination, ride type, fare, promo code used, trip status (requested, accepted, completed or cancelled), the time of the trip, and any rating you give. Driver information: if you register as a driver, we collect your full name, CNIC number, phone number, email, driving licence number, vehicle registration number, and your vehicle type, model, colour and plate number. Notifications: if you allow them, the app shows alerts on your phone about ride requests and ride updates. You can turn them off in Settings. Technical information: basic app and device data that our service providers generate to keep the app working and secure.

3. How we use your information

• To create and manage your account. • To match riders with drivers and complete rides. • To show the rider the driver's name, vehicle, plate number and phone number, and to show the driver the rider's name and phone number, so you can find and contact each other. • To calculate fares and apply promo codes. • To review and approve driver applications and help keep riders safe. • To show your ride history and ratings. • To improve the app, prevent misuse and fraud, and comply with the law.

4. Who we share it with

The other person on your ride: when a driver accepts a ride, the rider sees the driver's name, phone number, vehicle details and plate number, and the driver sees the rider's name, phone number, pickup and destination, and the rider sees the driver's live location until the ride ends. Approved drivers can also see the pickup, destination, fare and rider's name of ride requests that are still waiting for a driver. Your CNIC and driver documents are never shown to riders. Service providers that help us run the app: Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores account and trip data. To turn addresses into map locations and to draw routes, the app sends the address text you type and the pickup and destination coordinates to OpenStreetMap Nominatim and OSRM. These are third-party services with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety of users.

5. Storage and security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers. We use access rules so that riders can only see their own trips and profile, and driver documents can only be read by the driver who submitted them and by our administrators. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and trip history while your account is active, and afterwards for as long as needed for safety, dispute resolution and legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time (see "Your choices").

7. Your choices

You can update your name and phone number in Profile. You can turn off location access in the app Settings or in your phone settings. You can still type your pickup address by hand. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to delete your account, by contacting us. Some information, such as completed trips, may be kept where the law requires or allows it.

8. Children

Quetta Ride is not meant for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If you keep using the app after a change, you accept the updated policy.
