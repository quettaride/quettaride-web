2. What Quetta Ride is

Quetta Ride is a platform that connects riders who need a ride with independent drivers. Quetta Ride does not own vehicles or employ drivers and does not itself provide transport. The ride is a service between the rider and the driver.

3. Eligibility and accounts

You must be at least 18 years old. Give accurate information and keep your password private; you are responsible for everything that happens on your account. Each account is either a customer (rider) account or a driver account.

4. Drivers

To accept rides you must submit a driver registration and be approved by us. You must hold a valid driving licence and vehicle registration, keep your vehicle roadworthy, follow traffic laws, and give accurate details about yourself and your vehicle. We may approve, reject, suspend or remove a driver at any time. Drivers are independent and are not employees of Quetta Ride. Only accept rides you intend to complete.

5. Riders

Enter correct pickup and destination details, be ready at the pickup point, and treat drivers with respect. Do not ask a driver to break the law or to carry unsafe or illegal items.

6. Fares, payment and promo codes

The fare shown before you confirm a ride is an estimate based on the route distance and the ride type. Payment is currently made in cash to the driver. Promo codes are limited, can be used once per account, may expire, and may be withdrawn if misused.

7. Cancellations

A rider can cancel a ride while it is waiting for a driver or after a driver has accepted it. Repeated cancellations or no-shows may lead to restrictions on your account.

8. Ratings

After a ride, riders can rate the driver from 1 to 5 stars. Drivers see the average of their ratings. Do not abuse the rating system.

9. Safety and the SOS button

Your safety matters to us, but Quetta Ride cannot guarantee it. Before you get in, check the driver's name, vehicle and plate number against the details shown in the app. The SOS button opens your phone's dialler to call the Police Helpline (15). It is a shortcut only: it does not send your location and does not alert anyone for you. In an emergency, call the emergency services directly.
